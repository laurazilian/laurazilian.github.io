Research

Work, skills and digital change

How technological change reshapes skill demand, occupations and unemployment. My PhD looked at technological change and employment dynamics from a complex-systems perspective. Since then I have worked on labour market polarisation, digital skill requirements of occupations and digital inequality.

PolarisationDigital skillsSkill mismatchAI and work

Partnership, family and gender

Who partners with whom, and what this means for inequality. I study local mating markets, educational assortative mating and the rise of hypogamy, child health from a couple perspective, and the role of the family in women's STEM careers.

Assortative matingMating marketsGender & STEMCouples

Methods

Austrian register data, survey data and online job ads. I work with econometrics, spatial and network analysis, agent-based modelling and natural language processing, mostly in Python, R and Stata.
